01 / Professional introduction

From troubleshooting systems to engineering software.

Professional self-assessment · draft

My technical background in installation, maintenance, and troubleshooting taught me to approach problems methodically: investigate the cause, test possible solutions, and document the result. During my computer science coursework, I applied that mindset to programming, software testing, databases, algorithms, and secure design.

In CS 499, I am building on Travlr Getaways, an earlier full-stack application, by adapting its architecture to support the HappyBirthday.com concept. Rather than starting from scratch, I am preserving recognizable elements of the original application while improving design, algorithms, and data management.

My goal is to work in software development or data analytics, where I can combine hands-on problem solving with clear technical communication. This introduction will be expanded into the final professional self-assessment, including examples from other courses and experiences.
